<task id="2">
<title>Implement Core Trading Strategies</title>
<read_first>
- src/strategy/base.py
- src/indicators/calculator.py
</read_first>
<action>
Implement 4 core quantitative strategies:
1. `src/strategy/ma_crossover.py`:
   - `MACrossoverStrategy`: Fast EMA (e.g. 9 or 20) crossing Slow EMA (e.g. 21 or 50). BUY on golden cross, EXIT/SELL on death cross. Dynamic stop-loss based on 2x ATR.
2. `src/strategy/rsi_reversion.py`:
   - `RSIMeanReversionStrategy`: BUY when RSI crosses above 30 from oversold; EXIT/SELL when RSI crosses below 70 from overbought.
3. `src/strategy/orb.py`:
   - `OpeningRangeBreakoutStrategy`: Determines high/low of the first N bars (e.g. 15-minute or 30-minute range). BUY when bar closes above opening high with above-average volume; SELL/SHORT when bar closes below opening low.
4. `src/strategy/trend_following.py`:
   - `TrendFollowingStrategy`: Multi-factor confluence: EMA 20 > EMA 50 (trend), MACD line > Signal line (momentum), and RSI between 50 and 70 (healthy trend, not overextended).
</action>
<acceptance_criteria>
- All 4 strategy files implement the `Strategy` ABC
- Strategies produce validated `Signal` instances with confidence scores and indicator snapshots
</acceptance_criteria>
</task>
